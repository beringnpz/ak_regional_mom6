9.1 Simulated temperature summary

We used the most recent MOM6-NEP near-real-time hindcast extension, which runs through Aug 14, 2026, to analyze the seasonal evolution of surface and bottom temperature throughout the three management regions: Bering Sea, Aleutian Islands, and Gulf of Alaska.

9.1.1 Bering Sea

Winter 2026 conditions in the Bering Sea began very close to the long-term mean across the entire shelf. Then in March 2026, the southeastern shelf region cooled due to rapid ice advance, with surface and bottom temperatures dropping approximately 0.4 deg and 1.16 deg, respectively, below average (Figure 9.2 (a), Figure 9.2 (b)). As ice retreated in spring, surface temperatures in the southeastern region moved toward average values, but bottom temperatures remained below average, closely tracking the persistence signal from late April/early May. The EBS 2 deg cold pool index peaked at a near-average value of 0.78 in April, but decreased more slowly than the average, staying 0.08-0.11 above the mean through August (Figure 9.2 (c)). The EBS 0 deg cold pool index peaked well above the median, reaching nearly 0.5 in mid-April. However, this less than 0 deg water rapidly diminished in May, reaching value about 0.01 below the mean by mid June (Figure 9.2 (d)). In the northern Bering Sea, bottom temperatures remained near average throughout the year, while surface temperatures were warmer than average (and warmer than the conditions predicted by persistence alone).

This footprint of a smaller than average summer 0 deg cold pool but larger than average 2 deg cold pool can be seen in the July bottom temperature maps (Figure 9.1). The closest analog year from the hindcast suite (1993-2025) with this summer pattern of cold conditions in the southeastern region but neutral to warm conditions in the northern Bering Sea in the summer months is 2007 (Figure 9.4 (a)). However, the similarity decreases when looking at the full seasonal evolution of these anomalies (Figure 9.4 (b).

9.2 2026 persistence forecast evaluation

Our current MOM6-NEP forecast is a persistence-based forecast, meaning it simply overlays the anomaly from climatology from the end of the spring near-real-time hindcast extension (April 29, 2026) on climatological values. Here, we analyze how well this forecast performed in this current year.

Bottom temperature in the eastern Bering Sea tends to be well-predicted by persistence; this year, the cold anomaly across the southeastern shelf and warm anomaly in the northwestern region was well-predicted by the forecast. The northeastern shelf was still ice-covered at the time of the forecast snapshot; in this region, the realized hindcast showed warmer values in Norton Sound and a patch of much colder bottom water between Norton Sound and St. Lawrence relative to the neutral forecast conditions. The shallow inner shelf region, which is well-mixed and therefore more influenced by surface warming than the rest of the shelf, moved toward warmer temperatures than predicted, particularly in July. In the Gulf of Alaska and Aleutian Islands, the forecast captured the pattern of neutral to warm conditions in the southeast Gulf of Alaska and cool conditions in the western Gulf and eastern Aleutian Islands.

The persistence signal tends to have less predictive power for surface temperature than bottom temperature. The forecast predicted cold conditions in Bristol Bay and the Gulf of Alaska, neutral northern Bering Sea, and warm western Bering, with warmer conditions in the northeast Pacific Gyre. With the exception of the NBS region, these patterns were maintained through the summer, with some strengthening of the band of cool anomalies through the off-shelf Gulf of Alaska and Bering Sea basin. Conditions in the northern Bering Sea warmed considerably above the forecast, especially in July; this lower skill is expected due to the ice cover in that region at the time of forecast initialization.

Code 
[A, Grd] = akmapprep('cpopts', C, 'maskval', [1 3 4]);

ttarget = datetime(2026,6:8,1, 'format', ', yyyy/MM/dd');
nt = length(ttarget);
pltvars = ["tob", "tos"];
pltvname = ["Bottom temp. (\circC)", "SST (\circC)"];
np = length(pltvars);

vartype = ["value", "fcpersis", "anomaly"];
nv = length(vartype);

mapprops = {'mtick', '', 'pticks', ''};

% Plot realized value, predicted value, realized anomaly, and anomaly
% mismatch

h = plotgrid('size', [nt+1, np], 'mar', 0.05, 'sv', 0.05, 'sh', 0.05);
h.fig.Position(3:4) = [900 1062];
h.ax = subgridaxes(h.ax, 2,2);

cbloc = 'east';

for it = 1:nt
    for iv = 1:np

        axes(h.ax(1,1,it+1,iv));
        Tmp1 = readmom6mapslice(C, ttarget(it), 'vars', pltvars{iv}, 'vartype', 'value');
        htmp = plotmom6akmap(A, Tmp1, ...
            'var', pltvars{iv}, ...
            'vartype', 'value', ...
            'mapprops', mapprops, ...
            'cbloc', cbloc);
        reposition(htmp.cb, [0.5 0.8], [0 0], 'e');

        axes(h.ax(1,2,it+1,iv));
        Tmp2 = readmom6mapslice(C, ttarget(it), 'vars', pltvars{iv}, 'vartype', 'fcpersist');
        htmp = plotmom6akmap(A, Tmp2, ...
            'var', pltvars{iv}, ...
            'vartype', 'value', ...
            'mapprops', mapprops, ...
            'cbloc', cbloc);
        reposition(htmp.cb, [0.5 0.8], [0 0], 'e');

        axes(h.ax(2,1,it+1,iv));
        Tmp3 = readmom6mapslice(C, ttarget(it), 'vars', pltvars{iv}, 'vartype', 'anomaly');
        htmp = plotmom6akmap(A, Tmp3, ...
            'var', pltvars{iv}, ...
            'vartype', 'anomaly', ...
            'mapprops', mapprops, ...
            'cbloc', cbloc);
        reposition(htmp.cb, [0.5 0.8], [0 0], 'e');

        axes(h.ax(2,2,it+1,iv));
          
        Tmp4.(pltvars{iv}) = Tmp1.(pltvars{iv}) - Tmp2.(pltvars{iv});
        htmp = plotmom6akmap(A, Tmp4, ...
                    'var', pltvars{iv}, ...
                    'vartype', 'anomaly', ...
                    'mapprops', mapprops, ...
                    'cbloc', cbloc);
        reposition(htmp.cb, [0.5 0.8], [0 0], 'e');

        labelaxes(h.ax(:,:,it+1,iv), ["a)", "c)", "b)", "d)"], 'northwest');

        % Add predicted anomaly

        if it == 1
            fcanom = Tmp2.(pltvars{iv}) - (Tmp1.(pltvars{iv}) - Tmp3.(pltvars{iv}));
            axes(h.ax(2,1,1,iv));
            htmp = plotmom6akmap(A, struct(pltvars{iv},fcanom), ...
                    'var', pltvars{iv}, ...
                    'vartype', 'anomaly', ...
                    'mapprops', mapprops, ...
                    'cbloc', cbloc);
            reposition(htmp.cb, [0.5 0.8], [0 0], 'e');
        end


    end
end

lbls = pltvname + string(ttarget');
labelaxes(h.ax(1,1,2:end,:), lbls, 'northwestoutsideabove');

lblsfc = pltvname + ": forecast anomaly";
labelaxes(h.ax(2,1,1,:), lblsfc, 'northwestoutsideabove')


set(h.ax(2,2,:,:), 'colormap', cmocean('curl'));
delete(h.ax(1,:,1,:));
delete(h.ax(2,2,1,:));

archivepng(h.fig, esrfol, sprintf('fcpersis_vs_hindcast_%d', yr), '-r150');
